# codwawzpc launcher concepts

Eight image-generated proposals for the native launcher for **Call of Duty: World at War: Zombies, iPhone v1.5.0**. These are visual design mockups, not screenshots of an implemented launcher. Each explores the same primary actions: Play, Settings & controls, Saved games, and Import game.

| Concept | Visual direction | Reference basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [01 — The Last Barricade](01-last-barricade.png) | Concrete-wall menu beside a moonlit barricaded bunker; red painted steel primary action. | Nacht der Untoten architecture, original mobile title art, bunker survival atmosphere. |
| [02 — Outbreak Dossier](02-outbreak-dossier.png) | Cream intelligence folder, typewritten labels, clipped photographs of the four maps, faded olive canvas. | Original Shi No Numa loading-screen map and photo collage; companion to the RE4 and Degeneration launchers. |
| [03 — Der Riese Switchboard](03-der-riese-switchboard.png) | Industrial observation window and field-gray enamel control panel with mechanical keys and an analog instrument. | Der Riese machinery and the original World at War color studies; immersive hardware approach like the BioShock launcher. |
| [04 — Shi No Numa Field Radio](04-shi-no-numa-field-radio.png) | Expansive swamp outpost with timber, fog, and a low horizontal field-radio control panel. | Original Shi No Numa environment imagery and the iPhone map capture. |
| [04 variant — Shi No Numa with Wunderwaffe](04-shi-no-numa-field-radio-wunderwaffe.png) | Adds a large Wunderwaffe DG-2 with glowing blue electrical tubes on the foreground workbench, preserving the field-radio controls and swamp setting. | Edit of concept 04; an image-generated interpretation of the weapon. |
| [05 — 2009 Nightmare](05-2009-nightmare.png) | Distressed black, silver, white, and red title-menu graphics; stark vertical menu and undead soldier portrait. | Original iPhone branding and early World at War front-end layouts. |
| [06 — The Mystery Cabinet](06-the-mystery-cabinet.png) | An open timber Mystery Box becomes the interface, with a brass-and-red latch for Play and embedded inventory labels for utilities. | The early Mystery Box's wood, question marks and teddy-bear motif; original mobile branding and Nacht atmosphere. |
| [07 — Dead Reel](07-dead-reel.png) | A luminous wartime film frame with monochrome bunker imagery, sprocket edges, and a red admission-ticket Play control. | Original mobile graphic identity and airfield imagery, reinterpreted through period film media. The projector metaphor is a new design invention, not a later-map reference. |
| [08 — Verrückt Diagnostic](08-verruckt-diagnostic.png) | A porcelain X-ray viewing board, dark radiographs, ceramic tiles and crimson enamel controls. | The original mobile Verrückt capture and an artist's asylum lighting palette. The diagnostic lightbox and radiographs are new design metaphors, not game-asset reproductions. |
| [09 — Monkey Bomb maintenance rail](09-monkey-maintenance-rail.png) | Continuous swamp scene, seated Monkey Bomb and an inset dark-metal footer with quiet engraved controls and pilot lights. | Built-in image-generation edit of the user's October 22 launcher screenshot. The native implementation uses the same layout direction with real Tk controls. |

## Online references consulted

- [Thomas A. Szakolczay — World at War concept art](https://tas.artstation.com/projects/4a4Q8): artist portfolio containing an early marketing image, front-end UI flow, environment studies, and a mood color script. The artist identifies the early marketing direction as dating to 2007. The front-end sheet is an early design reference, not a claim about the shipped Zombies menu.
- [Damoun Shabestari — World at War Zombies](https://www.damounshabsdesign.com/call-of-duty-world-at-war-zombies): designer portfolio with the original mobile title image. The title screen's metallic lettering, white brush-written Zombies subtitle, red ink, fog and distressed vignette inform the branding. The page also includes fan artwork; that is not treated as official concept art.
- [Original-map loading-screen gallery](https://matthewstasiewski.weebly.com/maps.html): the Shi No Numa map, folded paper, handwritten notes, and monochrome character photographs inform the dossier's material treatment. This is a community-hosted reference.
- [Shi No Numa retrospective](https://gamerheadquarters.com/revisited/callofdutyzombiesshinonumarevisited.html): original-era swamp imagery used for timber outpost architecture, fog, and foliage. This is a secondary source, not the original artist's portfolio.
- [Der Riese loading-screen archive](https://www.callofdutyzombies.com/zombie-library/zombies-library/worldatwar/derriese/loading-screens-r62/): distinguishes World at War screens from the later Black Ops comic-book treatment. Some embedded image hosts are no longer dependable; those images were not supplied as generation inputs.
- [Pocket Gamer's November 16, 2009 review](https://www.pocketgamer.com/call-of-duty-world-at-war-zombies/review/): contemporary context for the mobile release's dilapidated bunker, cramped survival layout and repairable barricades.
- [Gabereel — Verrückt / Asylum Z](https://www.gabereel.com/pages/gallery/gallery-cod-zombies/asylum-z.html): a development artist's account of the map's lighting, with an asylum color-palette board. The palette image was inspected and supplied as a supporting color/lighting reference for concept 08. Its warm rust, reds, ochres and shadow values inform the surrounding environment; the porcelain lightbox is an original interpretation.
- [Call of Duty Wiki — Mystery Box](https://callofduty.fandom.com/wiki/Mystery_Box): cross-checked the wooden-box, question-mark and teddy-bear motifs. Search imagery included later Black Ops versions and replicas; those were not used as generation reference inputs. Concept 06 uses the original mobile branding and local Nacht capture as image references.

## Generation and references

The concepts are generated design studies. Original game screenshots, official
artwork downloads, private generation prompts and local input paths are not
included. The source links above document visual references without redistributing
them. Separate generated masters and their runtime exports live in
`launcher/artwork/sources/` and `launcher/assets/`.
